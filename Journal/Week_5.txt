I didn't make a walking sim this week. I wanted to try something else. A makeshift driving sim! It's really just a regular old walking sim but with car noises, obstacles on a course, and crash noises. I used the micro fps game template and got rid of most of the stuff I didn't need. It's incredibly simple. I had to mostly do research on how to make it so an audio plays whenever the player crashes since I'm not that great at C#. When I first made the game, I tried adding online unity assets for a car, and cones and stuff to make it look slightly more realistic, but I felt like I was overcomplicating it. It's a walking simulator that's supposed to focus on game audio! So that's exactly what I focused on!
