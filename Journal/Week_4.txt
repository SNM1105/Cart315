It's been a while. I created a snake game! I call it Poacher Snake. You dont have to deal with running into your own tail anymore. Instead, you have to survive being shot by evil poachers in the savannah. You're still a sneak, but now you eat meat and get shot at. You can eat the poachers. Every time they shoot you, they take a square off your body. Eating them or the meat grants one square back. I also added a shield that allows you to get hit by 3 bullets without losing any length. Poachers spawn in once every 4 seconds, and shoot every second. Shield spawns in every 15 seconds. The game itself is quite difficult, but I personally cant stop playing it. There's no end game. Just survival. I'm thinking of adding a timer to see how long you survive.
